
# Activity 5: AI Prompt Engineering

## Activity Description

This activity focused on exploring the use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools and understanding the importance of creating clear, detailed, and specific prompts to generate accurate and desirable results.

## What I Learned

Through this activity, I learned the importance of providing detailed and specific instructions when using AI tools to achieve my desired results. I realized that the quality of an AI-generated output greatly depends on how clearly the prompt communicates my expectations. One challenge I encountered was being absent during the activity, which required me to ask my classmates for guidance and clarification about the requirements. Despite this challenge, I was able to understand the purpose of the activity and appreciate the practical applications of AI. I believe that the knowledge I gained will be valuable in my future career, particularly in improving productivity, communication, and creative problem-solving.
